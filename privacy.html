Privacy

  • No accounts, no tracking cookies. Own Chickens is a static website. We do not run ads or set tracking cookies.
  • Analytics. No analytics are running right now. If we add analytics later, we will use a privacy-friendly, cookie-free service that counts page visits without building profiles of you, and we will update this page.
  • Email list. Our email list is not live yet. Until it is, signup forms do not send or store your address anywhere. When it launches, your email will be stored by our email provider only to send the checklist and occasional updates, and every email will include an unsubscribe link.
  • Local law finder. The city and ZIP you type are processed in your browser to search our small database. Fallback links open official-source searches on Google, Municode, or American Legal, which have their own privacy policies.
  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security.

Affiliate disclosure

Some product links on this site may become affiliate links, including Amazon Associates links. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, Own Chickens would earn from qualifying purchases.

Commissions never decide what we recommend. Our buying guides explain what to look for, what to skip, and when a cheaper or used option is good enough. We do not publish made-up test results, star ratings, or prices.

Accuracy and corrections

Chicken-keeping advice here follows common extension and USDA guidance, written for beginners. It is not veterinary or legal advice. Local law summaries list their official sources and the date we last checked them. If something is out of date, please report a correction.

Own Chickens is an educational starting point. Always verify current rules with your city or county before buying birds or building a coop.
