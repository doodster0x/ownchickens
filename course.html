Who it’s for

Beginners who want a plan before the first bird

  • Beginners anywhere you can legally keep hens on your own property: urban, suburban, or rural, in any state

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this the full year course?

No. This is the Year One Starter — the first roughly 90 days of planning: legal, budget, breeds, coop basics, plus a winter one-pager. The full outline (Your First Year With Backyard Chickens) is approved and will ship in pieces after we learn from these first buyers.

Do I need the free ownchickens.com tools?

Yes. They’re part of the homework. Lessons send you to the ZIP lookup, laws, cost calculator, breed quiz, and related guides. The course doesn’t replace the site; it teaches you how to use it in order.

Does this work where I live?

Yes, if you can legally keep hens on your property, in any state. The course shows you how to find your own local rules and what to adjust for cold winters or hot summers. Always confirm with your city or county.

What do I get immediately?

Access to the welcome video, Modules 0–4 materials (videos or narrated walks, plus PDFs and checklists), and the winter one-pager, delivered through the Gumroad product page once live.
