Local laws · Minnesota

Saint Paul chicken laws

City of Saint Paul requires a Backyard Chicken Keeping Permit. Tier One covers 1-6 hens; Tier Two covers 7-15 hens and needs a neighbor petition. Roosters are prohibited.

Last checked 2026-10-18 against official city sources. Rules change, so confirm with the city before buying birds.

This applies inside Saint Paul city limits. ZIP codes and mailing addresses can cross city lines, and nearby towns, villages, and counties have their own rules. HOA rules and leases can be stricter.

Before you apply

  • Tier One: submit an application, site plan, and payment, and notify all adjacent neighbors in writing.
  • Tier Two: submit an application, site plan, payment, and a neighbor petition with 75% approval (see the city page for the distance rule).
  • Coops over 200 square feet or that use electricity also need a building and/or electrical permit.

Key rules

  • Roosters are prohibited.
  • No more than 15 birds at a time.
  • Permitted coops are inspected by Animal Control officers every year.
