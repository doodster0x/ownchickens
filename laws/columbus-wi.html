Columbus chicken laws

City of Columbus, Wisconsin allows up to 4 chickens per lot with an annual license from the Common Council. Roosters and slaughter are prohibited.

Last checked 2026-10-18 against official city sources. Rules change, so confirm with the city before buying birds.

This applies inside Columbus city limits. ZIP codes and mailing addresses can cross city lines, and nearby towns, villages, and counties have their own rules. HOA rules and leases can be stricter.

Before you apply

  • Get the license before January 1 each year, or within 30 days of first getting chickens.
  • File the application with the City Clerk and pay the non-refundable application fee plus the license fee.
  • The city confirms the setup meets the ordinance before issuing the license.

Key rules

  • Chickens must have a covered and fenced enclosure and stay in it at all times.
  • Enclosures must be at least 25 feet from any neighboring residential structure and follow accessory-building zoning rules.
  • All stored feed must be kept in airtight, rodent-proof containers.
  • No slaughtering on the lot. City code also says chickens or other fowl may not be given away, bartered, or sold.
  • Full rules are in Sec. 14-19 of the Columbus Municipal Code.
