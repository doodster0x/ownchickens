Beloit chicken laws

City of Beloit allows up to 4 female chickens on single-family residential lots with an annual residential chicken permit. Roosters and on-site slaughter are prohibited.

Last checked 2026-10-25 against official city sources. Rules change, so confirm with the city before buying birds.

This applies inside Beloit city limits. ZIP codes and mailing addresses can cross city lines, and nearby towns, villages, and counties have their own rules. HOA rules and leases can be stricter.

Before you apply

  • Obtain an annual residential chicken permit from Planning and Building Services before acquiring chickens or building a coop or run.
  • Include proof of Wisconsin DATCP Livestock Premises Registration (Wis. Stat. 95.51 and ATCP 17).
  • Submit an accurately scaled drawing showing coop and run dimensions and distances to lot lines and the nearest adjoining principal structure.
  • If the applicant is not the parcel owner, the property owner must sign the application and have the signature notarized.

Key rules

  • Allowed on residential zoning lots containing one single-family dwelling unit (Beloit Code of General Ordinances Sec. 7.244).
  • Coop and run must be in the rear yard, at least 10 feet from the rear wall of your home, at least 20 feet from any principal structure on adjacent property, and at least 10 feet from lot lines (or 3 feet where contiguous to an alley).
  • Newly built or installed coops cannot exceed 24 square feet; attached runs cannot exceed 40 square feet or 1% of the rear yard, up to a maximum of 100 square feet.
  • Chickens must stay in the coop or run at all times and be secured inside the coop from sunset to sunrise.
  • Human food scraps may only be fed inside the coop (never in the run), all feed must be in rodent-proof containers, and slaughter is prohibited except at a licensed meat processing facility.
