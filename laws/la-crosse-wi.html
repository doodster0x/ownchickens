La Crosse chicken laws

City of La Crosse allows up to 8 chickens on single-family or duplex properties with an annual license. Roosters and slaughter are prohibited.

Last checked 2026-10-18 against official city sources. Rules change, so confirm with the city before buying birds.

This applies inside La Crosse city limits. ZIP codes and mailing addresses can cross city lines, and nearby towns, villages, and counties have their own rules. HOA rules and leases can be stricter.

Before you apply

  • Get a license within 30 days of acquiring chickens; the license year runs January 1 to December 31 and must be renewed each year.
  • File the application with the City Clerk, including a scale drawing of lot lines, coop and enclosure size, and distances to adjoining lot lines and homes.
  • Applications go to the Judiciary and Administration Committee for approval.
  • If a complaint was filed in the prior year, renewal requires written approval from at least 50% of owner-occupied neighbors within 100 feet.

Key rules

  • One-family or two-family dwellings only.
  • Chickens need a covered enclosure of at least 3 square feet per bird and must stay in it or a fenced backyard enclosure at all times.
  • No enclosure may be closer than 25 feet to any home on an adjacent lot.
  • Full rules are in Sec. 6-16 of the La Crosse Municipal Code.
