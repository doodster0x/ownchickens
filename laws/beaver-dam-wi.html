Beaver Dam chicken laws

City of Beaver Dam prohibits keeping chickens and other poultry within city limits under its 'certain creatures forbidden' animal ordinance (Municipal Code Chapter 6).

Last checked 2026-10-25 against official city sources. Rules change, so confirm with the city before buying birds.

This applies inside Beaver Dam city limits. ZIP codes and mailing addresses can cross city lines, and nearby towns, villages, and counties have their own rules. HOA rules and leases can be stricter.

Before you apply

  • Confirm whether your address is inside City of Beaver Dam corporate limits or in an unincorporated Dodge County township (unincorporated properties follow county/town zoning rather than city code).

Key rules

  • Beaver Dam Municipal Code Chapter 6 (Animals) lists chickens and poultry among creatures forbidden to be kept within the city.
  • While the Common Council amended the ordinance in 2021 to allow honeybee keeping, the prohibition on backyard chickens remains in effect.
