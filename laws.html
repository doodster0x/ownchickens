How we check

  • We read the city's own pages, ordinances, applications, and fact sheets, and link every source we use.
  • We record the date we last checked. Fees and rules change, so treat older dates with extra care.
  • We do not guess. If a rule is unclear on official sources, we say so and point you to the ordinance.
  • City rules apply inside city limits only. Villages, towns, townships, and counties have their own rules, and HOAs or leases can be stricter.
