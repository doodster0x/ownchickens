How to use these numbers

  • Budget assumes you have more time than cash: a used or DIY coop, salvaged materials, DIY feeders and waterers, and opening the coop door yourself. See The Budget List.
  • Middle assumes a kit coop or simple new-lumber build, basic store-bought gear, and an automatic door.
  • Premium assumes a heavy-duty coop and run, larger-capacity gear, and organic or specialty feed.
  • Whatever your tier, do not cheap out on predator-proofing, latches, and a safe heated waterer. Those protect the birds.

What is not included

  • Veterinary care. Find a vet who sees chickens before you need one.
  • Electrical work for a new outdoor outlet.
  • Building permits for large coops, which some cities require.
  • Treats, decorations, and upgrades.
  • Your time. Budget builds can take several weekends.
